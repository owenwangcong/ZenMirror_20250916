## RULE #5: SOLVE "AM I DOING THIS RIGHT?" WITH OBJECTIVE SCORING

### The Data
**Campaigns with "meditation quality score"**:
- Core: **Meditation quality % shown** → $2.25M
- Flowtime: **Focus score 0-100** → $428K
- Muse S: **Calm/neutral/active classification** → $3.5M

**Campaigns without objective scoring**: Lower emotional resonance, fewer repeat users.

### The Psychology
Meditation's #1 frustration: **"I don't know if I'm doing it right."**

Apps say "good job!" to everyone. Meditators know this is fake.

They crave **honest, objective feedback** even if it's sometimes unflattering.

### The Principle
> "Give them a number. Make it mean something. Let them improve it."

### ZenMirror Scoring System

**Primary Metric: "Coherence Score" (0-100)**
- Measures HRV coherence (heart rhythm smoothness)
- Updates every 10 seconds
- **50-69**: Beginning meditation state
- **70-84**: Good meditation state
- **85-100**: Deep meditation state

**Secondary Metrics**:
- **Breath Regularity**: How consistent breathing is
- **Stillness**: Motion detection (fidgeting reduces score)
- **RSA (Respiratory Sinus Arrhythmia)**: Heart-breath synchronization

**Session Summary**:
```
Today's Meditation: 23 minutes
Average Coherence: 78 (Good)
Time in Deep State: 12 min (52%)
Breath Steadiness: 85%

Your best session this week!
You're making progress.
```

### Campaign Messaging
**Video Voiceover**:
"Finally, know if your meditation is actually working. ZenMirror gives you a coherence score based on your heart rhythm. Watch it rise as you settle into calm. Track your progress over weeks and months. Turn meditation into a measurable skill."
